who we are:

Since 1995, FACT has shared the culture and traditions of the Philippines with VCU and Richmond. We are 400+ members of every background, held together by good food, big shows, and the bigs and littles who make a huge campus feel like home.

Three words we live by

  • Bayanihan bah-yah-NEE-hahn Unity. A whole community picking something up and carrying it together.
  • Kapamilya kah-pah-MEEL-yah Family. Not the one you were born into, the one that saves you a seat.
  • Kalayaan kah-lah-YAH-ahn Freedom. To create, to perform, and to be exactly who you are.

Our mission is to serve VCU and Richmond by advocating for the Filipino American community through social, political, cultural and educational action.

        Frequently asked questions

        Do I have to be Filipino to join?

        No. FACT has always been open to every VCU student, whatever your background. Come for the food, stay for the people.

        What is a GBM?

        A general body meeting. Each one has a theme, games and the news for the weeks ahead. The last one was Tribes night in the Commons Theater.

        I can't dance. Is that a problem?

        Not even a little. There are intramural teams, service projects, socials, a photo crew and plenty of people who just come to hang out.

        Where do I find the newest info?

        Instagram first, then the GroupMe. Every form and sign-up lives in our Linktree.

        FACT is a safe space

        We hold a zero tolerance policy, and anyone can report a concern to our senior advisors without giving their name.
